About us

At Prana.ai, we believe that technology should feel alive. We’re not just building digital models or avatars, we’re creating AI personalities with purpose, story, and soul.

Prana.ai is India’s first AI Influencer House, born from a simple but powerful idea: What if brands could work with creators who are always available, fully brand-safe, multilingual, and infinitely scalable yet still feel human?

We merge the precision of generative AI with the heart of storytelling to bring this vision to life.

Each Prana persona is designed like a real influencer, with distinct identities, emotions, and cultural depth — crafted to connect authentically with audiences across the world.
